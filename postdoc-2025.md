# Postdoc Position in Continuous Optimization

The Faculty of Mathematics at the University of Vienna is offering a fully funded postdoc position in the field of Continuous Optimization with applications in Machine Learning, Optimal Transport, Randomized Linear Algebra, and related areas.

## About the Position
The specific research project will be developed collaboratively with the selected candidate, allowing for flexibility to align
with your interests and expertise. 

### Key Details
- **Location**: University of Vienna, Faculty of Mathematics
- **Duration**: 1–2 years (full-time contract)
- **Salary**: approx. 4900 EUR per month (gross).
- **Supervisor**: Yura Malitsky 

## Requirements
We are looking for a highly motivated candidate with the following qualifications:
- **Good publication record**.
- **Strong background** in linear algebra, analysis, probability theory, and optimization. 
- **Good programming skills**, preferably in Python or Julia.  
- **Proficiency in English** (both written and spoken).  

## How to Apply
To apply, please send the following documents to [yurii.malitskyi@univie.ac.at](mailto:yurii.malitskyi@univie.ac.at) with the subject line "Postdoc position 2025"
1. **Curriculum Vitae (CV)**  
2. **Motivation Letter**: Explain why you are interested in pursuing this position.
3. **Transcript of Grades**: Include a copy of your academic transcript (bachelor’s and master’s degrees).
   
## Application Deadline: October 1, 2025

If you have any questions about the position, feel free to reach out via email. We look forward to receiving your application!
